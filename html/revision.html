Computer Science Revision

Computer Science is the study of how data is represented, processed and communicated using computer systems. In OCR A Level Computer Science, students develop both practical programming skills and a deeper understanding of the theory behind computers, including algorithms, data structures, computer architecture, networking and cybersecurity. The course also encourages problem solving and computational thinking, allowing students to design efficient solutions to real-world problems while considering the ethical, legal and environmental impacts of technology.

One important thing about OCR A Level Computer Science is that it is very theory-heavy, even though many people expect it to mostly be programming.
